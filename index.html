Our Legacy of Quality

Rooted in Erode's
textile heritage

Three decades of trust, quality, and commitment to India's textile heritage.

Sri Laxmi Traders has been a cornerstone of Erode's thriving textile industry since 1985. As a family-owned manufacturing and wholesale business, we bridge the gap between master weavers and businesses across India — delivering authentic handloom and power-loom fabrics at competitive wholesale prices.

Our deep relationships with weaver communities ensure you receive fabrics of unmatched quality, with the authenticity and craftsmanship that Erode is renowned for worldwide.
